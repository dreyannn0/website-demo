Tic-Tac-Toe

📖 How to Play / Rules

Engage in the timeless classic of Tic-Tac-Toe. Two players compete on a 3x3 grid, alternating turns to place their X or O marks. The objective is simple: align three of your symbols vertically, horizontally, or diagonally before your opponent does. If the grid fills up with no winner, it's a tie.

Retro Snake

📖 How to Play / Rules

Getting Started

Hit Enter or click the Start Game button below to immediately launch into the game. No delays, no countdowns!

Gameplay Mechanics & Rules

  1. Continuous Movement: The snake cannot be stopped; you are only in charge of steering.
  2. Scoring & Growth: Eating a food block adds 10 points to your score and extends your tail by one segment.
  3. Speed Scaling: Prepare yourself! As you cross specific score thresholds, the snake's speed will noticeably increase.
  4. Game Over Conditions: Colliding with the outer walls or intersecting your own body ends the run immediately.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys / WASD: Adjust the snake's direction.
  • Enter: Launch a fresh game.
  • Space / Escape: Pause and resume the action.
  • M: Toggle audio effects on or off.

    Tetris

    📖 How to Play / Rules

    Basic Rules

    • The Goal: Prevent the stacking blocks from reaching the very top of the screen.
    • Clearing Lines: Rotate and move falling pieces to create a solid horizontal line of blocks with no empty spaces. Completed lines disappear, freeing up space and earning you points.
    • Scoring: Clearing one line gives 100 points, two lines give 300, three lines give 500, and clearing four lines at once with a straight piece (a "Tetris") gives the highest reward.
    • Game Over: If your stack of blocks reaches the top of the playing field and blocks a new piece from entering, the game ends.

    Standard Controls

    • Left/Right Arrow Keys: Move the piece sideways.
    • Up Arrow: Rotate the piece.
    • Down Arrow: Soft drop (make the piece fall faster).
    • Spacebar: Hard drop (instantly lock the piece in place).
    • C Key: Hold/swap the current piece into the storage queue for later use.

    Arkanoid Ultimate

    📖 How to Play / Rules

    Basic Rules

    • The Goal: Play Arkanoid by controlling a small paddle called the Vaus to bounce an energy ball and break all the colored blocks on the screen.
    • Move the paddle: Slide the Vaus left and right at the bottom of the playfield to keep the ball from falling into the bottom pit.
    • Control angles: Bounce the ball off the edges of the Vaus for sharper angles or the center for shallower bounces to target hard-to-reach bricks.
    • Clear the stage: Destroy every standard colored brick to advance to the next level. You lose a life if the ball drops past your paddle.

    Power-Up Capsules

    Smashing specific blocks releases spinning letter capsules that upgrade your Vaus:

    • Orange (S): Slows down the speed of the energy ball.
    • Pink (P): Opens a warp escape to advance straight to the next round.
    • Yellow/Green (C): Lets you catch the ball on your paddle and release it when ready.
    • Red (L): Equips the Vaus with laser firing capabilities to shoot bricks directly.
    • Light Blue (D): Splits your single energy ball into three separate particles.
    • Dark Blue/Purple (E): Widens the physical size of the Vaus paddle.
    • Grey (B): Awards an extra life.
